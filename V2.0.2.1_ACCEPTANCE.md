**Status:** ACCEPTED

## Acceptance basis

Virginia at Nebraska was used as the principal acceptance matchup because v2.0.2 had exposed an over-aggressive power-to-margin conversion (Virginia -26.1).

The v2.0.2.1 candidate changed margin authority to refreshed Power difference at 80.0 game points per 1.0 Power plus explicit 2.2 HFA, retained PSCORE as symmetric scoring-shape only, replaced the biased quarter snapper with a mean-preserving discrete football-score PMF, recalibrated pregame/live win probability to that same discrete distribution, and made overtime resolution symmetric.

### UVA at Nebraska deterministic target
- Virginia -14.9
- Projected score approximately Virginia 34, Nebraska 19
- Displayed Virginia win probability 91.8%

### Broader W2 reasonableness witness
Using 15 exact W2 DraftKings holdout matchups from the existing W2 witness board, v2.0.2.1's average absolute modeled spread was 17.71 versus 19.87 for the witness market. The prior v2.0.2 architecture averaged 27.29 on the same set. Candidate spread MAE was approximately 5.93 versus approximately 11.95 for v2.0.2.

This witness is a reasonableness check only; the market board was not used as production authority for the simulator.
